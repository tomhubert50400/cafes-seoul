2. Verify: Page shows signup form with "Create account" title
3. Enter a valid email (use a real email you can access)
4. Enter a password - verify password strength meter appears and updates

6. Verify: Redirected to home page (or shows success message)
7. Check your email inbox for verification email
8. Click the verification link in email
9. Verify: Redirected to home page and logged in

**Test Login Flow:**
1. Navigate to http://localhost:3000/login
2. Enter the verified email and password
3. Click "Log in"
4. Verify: Redirected to home page

**Test Logout Flow:**
1. While logged in, navigate to http://localhost:3000/login or http://localhost:3000/signup
2. Verify: Logout button is visible in the header (to the right of logo)
3. Click the "Log out" button
4. Verify: User is logged out and redirected to /login
5. Verify: Logout button is no longer visible (since user is logged out)

**Test Validation:**
1. On signup, try submitting empty form - see validation errors
2. On login, enter wrong password - see "Invalid email or password" error
3. Toggle password visibility - works on both forms

**Test i18n:**
1. Switch language to Korean
2. Navigate to /login and /signup
3. Verify text is in Korean (including logout button shows "로그아웃")

**Test Resend Verification:**

2. Try to log in with that account
3. Verify: Error says "Please verify your email first" with resend option
